Project overview

Project context & objective

This university capstone investigated how transaction, demographic and seasonal retail information can be prepared together to support reporting and analytical decisions. It connects data engineering, predictive modelling and business intelligence in one workflow.

Retail analysis depends on understanding transactions, customer patterns and seasonal factors across datasets. The capstone examined how multiple sources could be organised for clearer business reporting.

Retail datasets often describe different aspects of the same business. Transaction histories show purchasing activity, while demographic and seasonal information provides additional context. To interpret them together, analytical work must clarify the time periods, source fields and data definitions.

Methodology

From inputs to insights

The following stages explain the workflow and the technical decisions behind this project.

01

Data preparation and integration

The project combined transaction, demographic and seasonal datasets through extraction, transformation and loading (ETL). Cleaning and preparation were necessary before joining or comparing different kinds of information.

02

Analysis and modelling

Python, SQL and scikit-learn supported data analysis and predictive-modelling exploration. Modelling follows the preparation stage because inconsistent inputs can undermine the usefulness of predictions.

03

Dashboard presentation

Power BI and Tableau were used to present results in visual form. The aim was to make analytical patterns and relationships more accessible for business interpretation.

04

Communicating findings

The final analytical workflow brought data preparation, modelling and visual reporting together, illustrating how a question can move from source records to decision-support views.
