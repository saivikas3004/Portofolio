University capstone / Project case study

Integrated Retail Analytics & Optimisation

Connecting business data to decisions

01 / The problem

Retail analysis depends on understanding transactions, customer patterns and seasonal factors across datasets. The capstone examined how multiple sources could be organised for clearer business reporting.

02 / My approach

  1. Prepared and combined transaction, demographic and seasonal data through ETL and data-cleaning workflows.
  2. Applied predictive-modelling methods to explore relationships and possible trends.
  3. Presented analytical findings through Power BI and Tableau dashboards.

03 / Tools & methods

PythonSQLPower BITableauExcelscikit-learn

04 / Systems Analyst perspective

Data integration, information quality, reporting requirements and the design of useful decision-support outputs.

Evidence note: This is an academic capstone, not a production retail deployment. No independently verified commercial impact or numerical outcome is claimed.
