Research publication

Prediction of Electricity Consumption using Adaptive Neuro-fuzzy Inference System (2020).

Authors: Sai Vikas Malladi, Sardar Maran P., and Venkat Sankalp Mandava.

The research explores electricity consumption forecasting using an adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system (ANFIS), considering historical consumption and weather-related inputs. The available SSRN record identifies this as a publicly posted research paper; it does not independently establish peer-reviewed journal publication.

Read paper on Academia.edu ↗   ·   View SSRN research record ↗

The problem

Energy-demand forecasts require organised inputs and clear evaluation of model behaviour. The study examined a neuro-fuzzy approach to consumption forecasting.

My approach

  1. Prepared and normalised electricity-consumption information and related inputs.
  2. Explored an adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system (ANFIS) using MATLAB and Python.
  3. Examined modelling and validation as part of the forecasting workflow.

Tools & methods

MATLABPythonANFIS

Systems Analyst perspective

Defining analytical requirements, evaluating model assumptions and translating forecasts into practical planning discussions.

Evidence note: Academic research only. No live utility integration, commercial deployment or measured improvement is claimed.
Project outcome

What this project produced

A co-authored 2020 research paper examining electricity consumption forecasting using an adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system.

What I learned

Model evaluation should distinguish exploratory research from validated operational deployment.
